The foundation

My journey began with an innate curiosity about how things work. Growing up in a world increasingly shaped by technology, I was fascinated by the systems that power our daily lives. This curiosity led me to pursue Information Technology at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, where I began formalizing my understanding of systems, networks, and security.

What excites me about technology is its potential to solve real human problems. Whether it's securing data from threats or building systems that help people work more efficiently, I believe technology should serve as an enabler rather than a complication.
